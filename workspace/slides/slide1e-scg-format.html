JAMS Philosophy

"Actually Minimalist JSON Alternative" — Most alternatives have longer specs than JSON itself. JAMS builds on JSON's foundation, reducing quote noise while keeping parsing simple.

SCG Design Goals

Graph-native structure with explicit @nodes and @edges. Single-char status codes (P/I/D/B). Pipe-delimited rows. Git-friendly diffs.

Benefits

Compact text format. Human-editable. Git-friendly diffs. Reduced context for LLMs.
